# Wellington (Te Whanganui-a-Tara)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `wellington`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Wellington Region
* **Vibe**: The "Coolest Little Capital in the World" — culinary powerhouse, craft beer haven, indie cinema, museum capital, and wild coastal winds.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Museum of New Zealand Te Papa Tongarewa**: Free national museum with groundbreaking interactive exhibits (Gallipoli: The Scale of Our War, Māori taonga).
- **Wellington Cable Car & Botanic Garden**: Historic red funicular rising from Lambton Quay to Kelburn lookout and lush gardens.
- **Cuba Street & Hannahs Laneway**: Bohemian pedestrian street with Bucket Fountain, street buskers, indie boutiques, and artisan chocolate/craft beer alleyway.
- **Mount Victoria Lookout**: 196m lookout over Wellington Harbour, airport runway, and city skyline.
- **Zealandia Ecosanctuary**: World's first fully-fenced urban ecosanctuary restoring 500 years of native wildlife (kiwi, takahē, kākā, tuatara).
- **Wētā Workshop**: Film visual effects and props tour in Miramar (The Lord of the Rings, Avatar).

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Customs by Coffee Supreme* (Specialty coffee and gourmet sourdough toasties on Ghuznee St)
- *Flight Coffee Hangar* (Legendary coffee tasting flights)
- *Garage Project Taproom (Aro St)* (Trailblazing experimental craft beer)
- *Loretta & Ortega Fish Shack* (Cuba St dining and legendary coastal seafood)

## Practical Notes:
- "You can't beat Wellington on a good day" — but expect high winds ("Windy Welly"). Bring a structured windproof jacket instead of an umbrella.
- Super compact and walkable city center.
