# Tauranga & Mount Maunganui (Tauranga Moana / Mauao)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `tauranga`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Bay of Plenty
* **Vibe**: Sunny coastal paradise, premier surf culture, beach lifestyle, harbour dining, and the iconic extinct volcanic cone of Mauao.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Mount Maunganui (Mauao)**: Iconic 232m volcanic summit climb (45 mins) offering 360° views of the Pacific Ocean and Matakana Island, plus flat base track.
- **Main Beach & Pilot Bay**: World-famous surf beach on the ocean side, and calm harbour waters on Pilot Bay for paddleboarding and sunset fish & chips.
- **Tauranga Waterfront & The Strand**: Vibrant dining strip, children's tidal steps, and historic cargo port.
- **Papamoa Beach**: Endless stretch of white sand and dunes, popular for beach walks and surfing away from the main Mount strip.
- **McLaren Falls Park**: 190 hectares of parkland, lake walks, glowworms at dusk, and scenic waterfall.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Mount Bistro* (Award-winning coastal cuisine and seafood)
- *Eight Ounce Coffee / Central Deli* (Top Mount Maunganui flat whites)
- *Rice Rice Baby* (Vibrant Vietnamese street food in Mount Maunganui)
- *The Rising Tide* (Craft beer taproom & dumpling house with Mount Brewing Co. beers)

## Practical Notes:
- Peak summer (Dec–Jan) is bustling with holidaymakers and surfers. Parking near Mount Main Beach fills early in the morning.
