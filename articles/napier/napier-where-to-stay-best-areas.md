# Where to Stay in Napier & Hawke’s Bay: Best Art Deco Areas & Winery Lodges

Rebuilt in dazzling Spanish Mission, Stripped Classical, and Art Deco styles following the 1931 earthquake, Napier (Ahuriri) is the architectural jewel of New Zealand. Positioned on the sunny eastern coast of the North Island, the city offers a Mediterranean climate, world-renowned Gimblett Gravels wine estates, and seaside boulevards shaded by colossal Norfolk Island pines.

Choosing where to stay in Hawke’s Bay allows you to tailor your holiday between three distinct lifestyles: staying in a 1930s Art Deco hotel along Marine Parade, relaxing in a maritime warehouse apartment in historic Ahuriri, or retreating to a luxury cottage nestled among vineyard rows. ## 1. Napier CBD & Marine Parade: Best for First-Timers & Art Deco Lovers

The heart of the 1930s architectural precinct, running parallel to the Pacific Ocean along Marine Parade.

- **The Vibe**: Timeless vintage elegance. Stroll beneath towering Norfolk pines past the Tom Parker Fountain, the Sound Shell, the National Aquarium of New Zealand, and world-famous cafes (*Mister D*).
- **Pros**: Immediate walking access to all guided Art Deco architectural tours, boutique shops, and urban restaurants; beautiful sunrise ocean views over Hawke Bay.
- **Cons**: Marine Parade beach is a steep pebble and gravel beach unsuitable for swimming; street parking is metered during retail hours.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *The County Hotel* (5-Star Edwardian luxury hotel built in 1909, one of the few buildings to survive the 1931 earthquake)
  - *Art Deco Masonic Hotel* (Iconic Art Deco landmark hotel positioned directly opposite the Sound Shell)
  - *Express Tel* (Modern, comfortable motel options near the central boulevard)

---

## 2. Ahuriri Historic Port: Best for Foodies & Maritime Charms

Located just 5 minutes northwest of the CBD around the sheltered inner harbour.

- **The Vibe**: Napier’s hippest historic precinct. Former 19th-century industrial wool warehouses converted into chic boutique hotels, gin distilleries, seafood bistros (*The Fish Shop*), and craft taprooms.
- **Pros**: Outstanding waterfront dining and sunset views right at your doorstep; relaxed seaside atmosphere; safe swimming at Ahuriri Beach.
- **Cons**: Requires a short 5-minute drive, bike ride, or 30-minute flat walk to reach the central Art Deco shopping streets of the CBD.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *The Crown Hotel Napier* (Upscale 4.5-star heritage hotel overlooking the waterfront with luxury apartment suites)
  - *Navigate Seaside Hotel & Apartments* (Sleek, contemporary boutique accommodation near Ahuriri Beach)
  - *Ahuriri Heritage Lofts* (Spacious converted woolstore industrial apartments)

---

## 3. Havelock North & Vineyard Estates: Best for Luxury & Wine Touring

Situated 20 minutes south of Napier beneath the dramatic limestone cliff of Te Mata Peak.

- **The Vibe**: An upscale, leafy village surrounded by prestigious Hawke’s Bay vineyards (Craggy Range, Black Barn, Elephant Hill).
- **Pros**: World-class vineyard dining, luxury day spas, private cottage rentals, and immediate access to Te Mata Peak hiking and cycling trails.
- **Cons**: You must drive 20 minutes to reach central Napier attractions.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Craggy Range Luxury Lodges* (Award-winning 5-star vineyard lodges with private courtyards beneath Te Mata Peak)
  - *Black Barn Retreats* (Bespoke luxury cottages set among working olive groves and vineyards)
  - *Porters Boutique Hotel (Havelock North Village)* (Chic 4.5-star hotel in the village center)

## Parking & Cycling Logistics for Hawke's Bay

- **The Rotary Pathway Cycle Network**: Hawke’s Bay features over 200 kilometres of flat, off-road paved bicycle trails connecting Napier CBD, Ahuriri, and rural wineries. Many hotels provide complimentary cruiser bikes.
- **Rental Car Value**: Having a car is highly recommended if you wish to tour cellar doors in the Gimblett Gravels, drive up Te Mata Peak, or visit the gannet colony at Cape Kidnappers.

## Where to Stay in Napier: Straight Answers

**How many days should I stay in Napier?**
**2 to 3 days** allows you to do an Art Deco walking tour, spend a full day cycling between Hawke’s Bay cellar doors, and hike the panoramic ridge of Te Mata Peak.

**Is it better to stay in Napier CBD or Ahuriri?**
Stay in **Napier CBD** if you want to be immersed in 1930s Art Deco architecture and walk to shops and museums. Choose **Ahuriri** if you prefer a boutique maritime village feel, sunset drinks over the fishing harbour, and trendy cafes.

**How far in advance should I book for the Art Deco Festival?**
If visiting during the annual **Art Deco Festival** (held every February), book **9 to 12 months in advance**. The entire region sells out almost a year ahead for this world-famous vintage festival.
