# Gisborne (Tūranganui-a-Kiwa)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `gisborne`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | East Coast / Tairāwhiti
* **Vibe**: First city in the world to see the sunrise, world-class surf breaks, Chardonnay capital, deep Māori cultural roots.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Wainui Beach & Makorori Beach**: Renowned surf breaks with golden sand and uncrowded shores.
- **Titirangi (Kaiti Hill) Lookout**: Sweeping views of Poverty Bay, harbor, and Young Nick's Head.
- **Rere Rockslide & Rere Falls**: 60m natural smooth rock waterslide (ride with bodyboards/inflatables) and picturesque waterfall.
- **Eastwoodhill Arboretum**: National Arboretum of New Zealand with over 25,000 species of exotic and native trees.
- **Dive Tatapouri**: Reef-walking tour with wild stingrays and eagle rays.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Raglan Roast Gisborne* (Lively espresso spot on Wainui Road)
- *Ussco Bar & Bistro* (Former shipping company building, upscale East Coast dining)
- *Sunshine Brewery* (Legendary local craft brewery with woodfired pizza)
- *Matawhero Wines & Millton Vineyards* (Historic cellar doors tasting world-class biodynamic wines & Chardonnay)

## Practical Notes:
- Laid-back remote coast feel. Driving around East Cape (State Highway 35) is one of NZ's great scenic remote coastal drives.
