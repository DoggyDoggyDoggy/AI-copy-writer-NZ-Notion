# Napier (Ahuriri)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `napier`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Hawke's Bay
* **Tier**: `Tier 2: Regional Thematic Specialist`
* **Primary Archetype**: Art Deco capital of the world, 1930s architectural preservation, Hawke's Bay wine country (Syrah & Chardonnay), Mediterranean sunshine, coastal cycle trails.

---

## 🎯 Topic Compatibility & Feasibility Matrix:
* **Best Topics & Strengths**: Art Deco walking & vintage car tours, Hawke's Bay cellar doors & vineyard dining (Mission Estate, Church Road), gourmet bakeries & injection doughnuts (Mister D), coastal cycle path touring, Marine Parade & Norfolk pines, Te Mata Peak panoramas, Cape Kidnappers gannet colony.
* **Limitations & Banned Angles**: Do not write "rugged alpine hiking" or "dense public transit network". Public transit is limited; cycling or car is essential for wineries.

---

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Art Deco Historic Quarter**: Downtown Emerson & Tennyson streets rebuilt after 1931 earthquake with sunbursts, zigzags, and Māori motifs.
- **Marine Parade & Tom Parker Fountain**: Coastal promenade lined with Norfolk pines and viewing platform.
- **Ahuriri Historic Port & Wool Stores**: Converted wool warehouses, art galleries, and gin distilleries on Ossian St.
- **Te Mata Peak**: 399m dramatic limestone ridge overlooking Heretaunga Plains.
- **Hawke's Bay Cycle Trails**: 200+ km of flat off-road limestone paths linking wineries.

---

## ☕ Local Kai & Coffee Anchors:
- *Mister D* (Famous for warm freshly fried brioche doughnuts with injection syringes on Tennyson St)
- *Mission Estate Winery* (NZ's oldest operational winery established in 1851 on Church Rd)
- *FG Smith Eatery* (Converted wool warehouse dining in Ahuriri)
- *Brave Brewing Co.* (Top Hawke's Bay craft beer taproom in nearby Hastings)
