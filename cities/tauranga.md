# Tauranga & Mount Maunganui (Tauranga Moana / Mauao)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `tauranga`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Bay of Plenty
* **Tier**: `Tier 2: Regional Thematic Specialist`
* **Primary Archetype**: Sunny coastal beach lifestyle, iconic volcanic cone of Mauao, premier surf breaks, Pilot Bay paddleboarding & sunsets, craft brewery taprooms.

---

## 🎯 Topic Compatibility & Feasibility Matrix:
* **Best Topics & Strengths**: Surf culture & beach breaks (Main Beach, Papamoa), coastal summit hiking (Mauao Summit & Base Track), Pilot Bay harbor paddleboarding & fish and chips, craft beer taprooms (The Rising Tide / Mount Brewing), fresh coastal dining (avocados, kiwifruit, snapper).
* **Limitations & Banned Angles**: Do not write "historic Victorian stone architecture" or "alpine snow sports". Peak summer (Dec–Jan) has intense beach traffic and tight parking around the Mount.

---

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Mount Maunganui (Mauao)**: 232m extinct volcanic cone with 45-min summit hike and 3.4km scenic coastal base loop track.
- **Main Beach & Pilot Bay**: World-famous surf beach on ocean side, calm harbor waters on Pilot Bay.
- **Tauranga Waterfront & The Strand**: Vibrant dining strip and tidal steps along the harbor.
- **Papamoa Beach & Hills**: Endless white sand dunes and Māori heritage pā trails.
- **McLaren Falls Park**: 190 hectares of lake parkland, waterfalls, and evening glowworms.

## 💡 Honest E-E-A-T Realities:
- Mauao is an ancestral mountain of deep spiritual significance to three local iwi. Keep strictly to marked walking tracks. Parking fills by 8:30 AM in summer.
