# Whangārei (Whangārei-terenga-parāoa)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `whangarei`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Northland
* **Vibe**: Subtropical riverside city, arts hub, gateway to Tutukaka Coast and the Far North.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Whangārei Falls (Otuihau)**: 26m waterfall set in native bush, easy loop track.
- **Hundertwasser Art Centre & Wairau Māori Art Gallery**: Iconic gold-domed building at Town Basin.
- **Mount Parihaka**: Highest lookout over the city and harbour, ancient pā site.
- **Town Basin Marina**: Waterfront promenade with craft shops, cafes, and boat docks.
- **Nearby Coast**: Tutukaka Coast (gateway to Poor Knights Islands diving), Matapōuri Bay (Mermaid Pools), Whale Bay.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *The Quay* (Town Basin waterfront dining)
- *Mokaba Cafe* (Town Basin coffee & brunch)
- *Fat Camel Cafe* (Israeli street food & falafel)
- *Nook Cafe* (Parua Bay views)

## Practical Notes:
- Subtropical climate: warm, humid summers, mild wet winters.
- Ideal base for diving Poor Knights Islands (one of the world's top temperate diving spots).
