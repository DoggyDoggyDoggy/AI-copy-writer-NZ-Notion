# Rotorua (Te Rotorua-nui-a-Kahumatamomoe)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `rotorua`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Bay of Plenty
* **Vibe**: Geothermal wonderland (geysers, mud pools, sulphur steam), heart of Māori culture, and world-class redwood mountain biking.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Te Puia & Whakarewarewa Geothermal Valley**: Home to Pōhutu Geyser (erupts up to 30m high), living Māori village, and NZ Māori Arts & Crafts Institute.
- **Whakarewarewa Redwoods Forest (Titokorangi)**: 160+ km of mountain bike tracks under towering Californian Redwoods, and Redwoods Treewalk (illuminated suspension bridges).
- **Polynesian Spa & Secret Spot Hot Tubs**: Geothermal mineral soaking pools overlooking Lake Rotorua or tucked in native forest.
- **Waimangu Volcanic Valley & Wai-O-Tapu**: World's youngest geothermal system, Champagne Pool, Lady Knox Geyser.
- **Kuirau Park**: Free central public park with boiling mud pools, steaming mineral foot baths, and walking tracks.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Capers Epicurean* (Beloved cafe for healthy brunch, gluten-free treats, and great coffee)
- *Atticus Finch* (Eat Streat shared plates, craft cocktails, and energetic evening vibe)
- *Ciabatta Bakery* (Famous Longdog sandwiches and authentic European sourdough)

## Practical Notes:
- The distinctive natural sulphur ("rotten egg") smell is completely normal and harmless due to geothermal activity.
