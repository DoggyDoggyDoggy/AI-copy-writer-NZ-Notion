# Christchurch (Ōtautahi)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `christchurch`
* **Island & Region**: South Island | Canterbury
* **Vibe**: The Garden City reborn — cutting-edge modern architecture, vibrant street art, Avon River punting, and gateway to the Southern Alps.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Christchurch Botanic Gardens & Hagley Park**: 160+ hectare green heart of the city along the winding Avon River (Ōtākaro).
- **Riverside Market**: Bustling indoor 7-day farmers market with artisan food stalls, local butchers, and international eateries.
- **Canterbury Museum & Arts Centre**: Gothic revival heritage buildings housing independent artisan studios, cinemas, and galleries.
- **Port Hills & Gondola**: Panoramic views over Lyttelton Harbour, Banks Peninsula, and the snow-capped Southern Alps.
- **New Regent Street**: 1930s Spanish Mission style pastel-coloured pedestrian street with trams, cocktail bars, and gelato.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *C4 Coffee Co.* (Industrial roastery and espresso bar)
- *Grizzly Baked Goods* (Famed sourdough loaves, doughnuts, and morning buns)
- *50 Bistro* (Upscale Canterbury lamb and Akaroa salmon dining)
- *Dux Central* (Historic converted craft beer precinct with dozens of taps)

## Practical Notes:
- Very flat city center, highly bikeable with shared electric scooters and dedicated bike lanes.
- Gateway airport for South Island road trips and TransAlpine train journey to Greymouth.
