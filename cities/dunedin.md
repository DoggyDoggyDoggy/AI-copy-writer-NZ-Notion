# Dunedin (Ōtepoti)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `dunedin`
* **Island & Region**: South Island | Otago
* **Tier**: `Tier 2: Regional Thematic Specialist`
* **Primary Archetype**: Victorian & Edwardian Scottish heritage, wildlife capital of NZ (albatross, yellow-eyed penguins, sea lions), vibrant student culture, craft beer pioneer.

---

## 🎯 Topic Compatibility & Feasibility Matrix:
* **Best Topics & Strengths**: Rare wildlife safaris (Royal Albatross Centre, Penguin Place, sea lions on Otago Peninsula), Victorian Gothic architecture (Larnach Castle, Dunedin Railway Station), world's steepest street (Baldwin St), craft beer heritage (Emerson's Brewery), indie student culture & street art.
* **Limitations & Banned Angles**: Do not write "tropical sun vacation" or "flat cycling city". Dunedin is hilly, and southern Antarctic weather fronts can bring sudden cold snaps even in summer.

---

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Otago Peninsula & Taiaroa Head**: Only mainland breeding colony of Royal Albatross, yellow-eyed penguins (*hoiho*), fur seals.
- **Baldwin Street**: World's steepest residential street (35% maximum gradient).
- **Larnach Castle & Gardens**: New Zealand's only castle, 1871 ballroom and turret views.
- **Dunedin Railway Station**: Flemish Renaissance architectural masterpiece with Royal Doulton porcelain tiles.
- **St Clair Beach & Heated Saltwater Pool**: Southern ocean surf break and heated outdoor ocean pool.
