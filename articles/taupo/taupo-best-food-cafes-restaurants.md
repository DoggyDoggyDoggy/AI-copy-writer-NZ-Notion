# Where to Eat in Taupō: The Ultimate Local Food & Coffee Guide

Set on the shores of a colossal supervolcanic crater lake and guarded by the snow-draped volcanic peaks of Tongariro National Park, Lake Taupō (Taupō-nui-a-Tia) is a town built on outdoor adventure and hearty, satisfying hospitality.

After a morning braving alpine winds on the Tongariro Crossing or kayaking to ancient rock carvings, nothing beats walking into a bustling, sun-drenched industrial cafe for fried chicken waffles, warm artisan sourdough pastries, or locally caught wild trout. Alongside our [Taupō Travel Guide](/blog/taupo-travel-guide) and [Taupō Budget Guide](/blog/taupo-on-a-budget-tips), here is your curated guide to eating and drinking well around the lake.

## Best Coffee & Morning Bakeries

Taupō’s morning cafe culture is energetic, stylish, and focused on exceptional specialty roasts.

### Storehouse (Runanga Street)
Located in an airy, industrial warehouse space on the edge of the industrial precinct.
- **The Vibe**: Polished concrete floors, indoor greenery, retro timber tables, and an open kitchen pumping out high-energy brunches.
- **The Signature Dishes**: Buttermilk fried chicken and crispy waffles drizzled with chilli maple syrup, creamy mushrooms on house-baked brioche, and expertly pulled flat whites using locally roasted Supreme and Kokako beans.

### Replete Food Company (45 Heuheu Street)
A multi-award-winning culinary anchor of downtown Taupō for over two decades.
- **The Specialty**: Renowned for having the best breakfast cabinet in the Central North Island. Think slow-roasted lamb and rosemary pies, smoked fish frittatas, and seasonal berry brioche buns.
- **The Pantry**: Browse their boutique retail shelves stocked with artisanal New Zealand olive oils, chutneys, and volcanic honey.

### Baked with Love (Gascoigne Street)
A dream bakery and cakery studio where everything is made from scratch with real butter and free-range eggs.
- **Must-Try**: Their gourmet handmade brioche doughnuts filled with chocolate ganache, passionfruit curd, or salted caramel, alongside fresh sourdough loaves.

## Casual Lunch & Iconic Lakeside Dining

Midday dining in Taupō revolves around lake views and relaxed local meeting spots.

### The Bistro (40 Tuwharetoa Street)
A cozy, European-inspired neighborhood bistro tucked away on Tuwharetoa Street.
- **The Cooking**: Chef Jude Messenger crafts unpretentious, flavour-driven dishes using prime Central Plateau ingredients.
- **Top Plates**: Pan-seared market fish with saffron potato mash, slow-braised Hawke's Bay lamb shoulder, and confit duck leg with cherry jus.
- **The Wine**: An exceptional list showcasing the best Syrah, Chardonnay, and Pinot Noir from neighbouring Hawke's Bay and Martinborough.

### Dixie Browns (Lakefront / Lake Terrace)
A bustling, high-energy lakefront dining room with front-row views across Lake Taupō toward the mountains.
- **The Offerings**: Generous all-day menus featuring gourmet burgers, stone-baked pizzas, and an expansive breakfast menu that runs from early morning until late afternoon.

### Two Mile Bay Sailing Club (Two Mile Bay)
Perched right on the water's edge at the end of the lakefront promenade.
- **The Vibe**: The ultimate summer hangout. Sit on the open-air wooden pier with your feet dangling over the clear lake water.
- **The Menu**: Woodfired pizzas, craft beer pints from Lakeman Brewing, and live acoustic music as the sun sets behind the Western Bays.

## Craft Breweries & Central Plateau Taps

### Lakeman Brewing Co. (Taupō / Western Bays)
Taupō’s legendary rogue craft brewery, using pure volcanic rain and bore water filtered through ancient pumice layers.
- **The Beers**: Acclaimed hop-heavy ales like *Primate APA*, *Taupo Pale Ale*, and *Big Hairy Monster*. Look for Lakeman on tap across local craft bars along Tuwharetoa Street.

## Eating in Taupō: A Few Honest Questions Answered

**What is Taupō most famous for food-wise?**
Wild freshwater trout and volcanic produce. Lake Taupō and the tributary rivers around Turangi are world-famous for wild rainbow and brown trout. Due to New Zealand conservation laws, wild trout cannot be sold commercially in restaurants—but if you catch one on a fishing trip, local bistros like *The Bistro* will gladly cook and prepare your catch for your dinner table!

**Is Taupō good for vegetarians and vegans?**
Yes. Modern cafes like *Storehouse* and *Replete* cater extensively to plant-based diners with creative grain bowls, vegan pancakes, and dairy-free smoothies. Most central bistros provide dedicated vegetarian menus.

**What's the average cost of eating out in Taupō?**
Expect to pay **$18–$24 NZD** for a cafe brunch with specialty coffee, **$14–$18 NZD** for a casual lunch or pizza slice by the lake, and **$55–$80 NZD per person** for a sit-down dinner with a glass of regional Hawke's Bay wine.
