# Palmerston North (Te Papa-i-Oea)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `palmerston-north`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Manawatū
* **Vibe**: Youthful university and research city, parklands, riverside cycle trails, wind turbine backdrops.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Victoria Esplanade**: Sprawling gardens, Peter Black Conservatory, duck ponds, miniature railway.
- **Te Apiti – Manawatū Gorge Walk**: Native forest tracks, dramatic gorge scenery, Māori warrior sculpture (Whatonga).
- **The Square (Te Marae o Hine)**: Central park with clock tower, ponds, and open green space.
- **New Zealand Rugby Museum**: Comprehensive national rugby history and interactive game zone.
- **Te Manawa Museum of Art, Science & Heritage**: Cultural and interactive science exhibitions.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Cafe Cuba* (Longstanding local institution on Cuba St, epic cakes and brunch)
- *Libre* (Mexican street food & tequila)
- *The Elm Cafe* (Historic villa setting, top seasonal breakfast)
- *Ebony Coffee Roastery* (Specialty coffee roastery)

## Practical Notes:
- Easy flat walking and cycling city.
- Located within easy driving distance to Ruahine and Tararua forest parks.
