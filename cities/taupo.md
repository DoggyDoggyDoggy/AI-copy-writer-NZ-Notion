# Taupō (Taupō-nui-a-Tia)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `taupo`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Waikato / Central Plateau
* **Tier**: `Tier 2: Regional Thematic Specialist`
* **Primary Archetype**: Colossal alpine crater lake (caldera), thunderous river rapids (Huka Falls), free thermal soaking streams (Otumuheke), gateway to Tongariro National Park.

---

## 🎯 Topic Compatibility & Feasibility Matrix:
* **Best Topics & Strengths**: Lake activities (sailing, trout fishing, kayaking to Mine Bay Māori carvings), natural waterfalls & rapids (Huka Falls, Aratiatia Rapids), free natural hot mineral streams (Otumuheke / Spa Park), tramping base for Tongariro Alpine Crossing, crater lake vistas.
* **Limitations & Banned Angles**: Do not write "urban subway network" or "bustling nightlife strip". Lake water is chilly outside summer; wind off the lake can be crisp year-round.

---

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Lake Taupō**: Australasia's largest freshwater lake, formed inside a massive supervolcano caldera.
- **Huka Falls**: 220,000 litres per second roaring through a 15m rock ravine into a turquoise pool.
- **Mine Bay Māori Rock Carvings**: 14m tall lakeside cliff carving of high priest Ngātoroirangi by Matahi Brightwell.
- **Otumuheke Stream / Spa Thermal Park**: Free natural hot geothermal stream merging into the Waikato River.
- **Aratiatia Rapids**: Hydroelectric spillway opening creating wild whitewater four times daily.
