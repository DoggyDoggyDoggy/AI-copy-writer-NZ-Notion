# Hamilton (Kirikiriroa)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `hamilton`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Waikato
* **Vibe**: Thriving river city, lush farmland hub, world-class themed gardens, gateway to Hobbiton & Waitomo Caves.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Hamilton Gardens**: Internationally awarded themed garden collections (Italian Renaissance, Japanese Garden of Contemplation, Traditional Māori Te Parapara Garden, Surrealist Garden). Free entry to some, paid enclosed gardens.
- **Waikato River Trails & River Pathways**: Paved scenic walking and cycling paths along NZ's longest river.
- **Waikato Museum & ArtsPost**: Māori culture, waka (canoe) exhibitions, and local art galleries.
- **Hamilton Lake Domain (Lake Rotoroa)**: Peaceful 3.8km loop walk popular with locals.
- **Nearby Day Trips**: Hobbiton Movie Set (Matamata), Waitomo Glowworm Caves, Raglan surf town (45 mins west).

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Rocket Coffee* (Pioneering Waikato specialty coffee roaster)
- *Hayes Common* (Neighbourhood eatery overlooking the river, stellar seasonal brunch)
- *Madam Woo* (Malaysian hawker food on Victoria St)
- *Duck Island Ice Cream* (The original flagship scoop shop of NZ's favourite gourmet ice cream)

## Practical Notes:
- Easy 1.5 hr drive from Auckland down the Waikato Expressway.
