# Where to Eat in Napier & Hawke’s Bay: The Ultimate Food & Wine Guide

Basking in more annual sunshine hours than almost anywhere in New Zealand, Hawke’s Bay is the nation's premier fruit bowl and oldest wine region. Between the Art Deco boulevards of Napier (Ahuriri), the historic wool sheds of the inner harbour, and the sun-drenched Syrah and Chardonnay vines of the Gimblett Gravels, this region is a feast for the senses.

Eating here is defined by boundless generosity: warm, golden brioche doughnuts injected with homemade curd, long winery vineyard lunches beneath 100-year-old fig trees, freshly hauled coastal seafood, and crisp ciders poured in restored industrial warehouses. Alongside our [Napier Travel Guide](/blog/napier-travel-guide) and [Napier Budget Guide](/blog/napier-on-a-budget-tips), here is your ultimate insider guide to dining across Napier and Hawke's Bay.

## Best Coffee & Morning Bakeries

Start your morning along Napier’s Art Deco streets with world-class coffee and boundary-pushing pastry craft.

### Mister D (47 Tennyson Street)
An iconic culinary landmark on central Tennyson Street.
- **The Cult Dish**: Warm, sugar-dusted Italian brioche doughnuts served with a syringe filled with chocolate ganache, raspberry jam, or vanilla custard ($7.50–$9 NZD). You inject the filling into the pastry yourself at the table.
- **The Coffee**: Exceptional flat whites pulled using locally roasted Hawthorne coffee beans.

### FG Smith Eatery (9A Ossian Street, Ahuriri)
Housed inside a soaring, converted historic wool warehouse in the trendy port suburb of Ahuriri.
- **The Atmosphere**: Industrial brick, vintage leather, lush indoor plants, and natural light streaming through high factory windows.
- **Signature Brunch**: Crispy potato rosti with house-smoked salmon, poached free-range eggs, and avocado, alongside fresh morning cinnamon scrolls.

### Crazy Good Coffee (Ahuriri)
A dedicated espresso bar for true coffee aficionados, roasting their own beans and serving silky, perfectly balanced flat whites and pour-overs.

## Casual Lunch & Iconic Cellar Doors

Lunch in Hawke’s Bay belongs among the vineyards and historic coastal ports.

### Mission Estate Winery & Restaurant (198 Church Road, Taradale)
Established by French Society of Mary Marist missionaries in 1851, this is New Zealand’s oldest operational winery.
- **The Setting**: A majestic colonial mansion perched on the Taradale hills overlooking sweeping vineyard slopes and the city of Napier.
- **The Dining**: Elegant French-Kiwi seasonal menus, including roasted Hawke's Bay lamb loin with parsnip purée, matched with Mission's award-winning *Jewelstone* Syrah and Chardonnay.

### Church Road Winery (150 Church Road, Taradale)
Right next door to Mission Estate, Church Road is a pioneer of Bordeaux-style red blends and single-vineyard Chardonnays.
- **The Experience**: Relax under the plane trees on the expansive winery lawn with an artisan cheese and charcuterie platter, or book a guided tasting in their underground heritage stone cellar.

### The Fish Shop (Ahuriri Historic Port)
Located directly opposite the fishing wharves in Ahuriri.
- **The Catch**: Fresh daily landings of Hawkes Bay tarakihi, gurnard, and flounder, lightly battered and served with crispy chips. Eat on the waterfront boardwalk with views of yachts and fishing trawlers.

## Top Dinner Spots & Evening Dining

As the sun sets behind the limestone ridge of Te Mata Peak, Napier’s dining rooms deliver sophisticated regional cooking.

### Pacifica Restaurant (209 Marine Parade)
Led by celebrated chef Jeremy Rameka, Pacifica is set inside an unassuming blue corrugated-iron beach cottage on Marine Parade.
- **The Concept**: Michelin-calibre contemporary Māori-inspired degustation dining.
- **The Food**: A surprise multi-course tasting menu centered on wild, locally foraged ingredients and native seafood—think smoked eel with dashi, paua (abalone) ravioli, and coastal venison with native horopito pepper. Booking weeks in advance is essential.

### Bistronomy (40 Hastings Street)
A lively, innovative contemporary bistro in central Napier.
- **The Food**: Playful, flavour-driven seasonal dining that showcases Hawke's Bay farmers and growers without pretension. Standouts include confit duck leg, charred octopus with nduja, and inventive seasonal desserts.

## Craft Breweries & Urban Cideries

### Brave Brewing Co. (Queen Street, Hastings / 15 mins from Napier)
Hawke’s Bay’s premier independent craft brewery taproom.
- **The Beers**: Acclaimed hop-forward IPAs like *Tigermilk IPA*, *The Hills Have IPA*, and crisp pilsners, accompanied by legendary smashed burgers.

### Paynter's Cider (Hastings Region)
Artisanal, slow-fermented ciders crafted from 100% Hawke's Bay heritage apples and pears with zero added sugar.

## Eating in Napier: A Few Honest Questions Answered

**What is Napier and Hawke's Bay most famous for food-wise?**
World-class Syrah and Chardonnay wines, artisan stone fruits, and injected doughnuts at *Mister D*. The Gimblett Gravels and Bridge Pa Triangle sub-regions produce some of the most acclaimed red wines in the Southern Hemisphere. 

**Is Napier good for vegetarians and vegans?**
Yes. Because Hawke's Bay is New Zealand's agricultural capital, fresh organic vegetables and fruits take center stage. Modern bistros like *Bistronomy* and *FG Smith* offer exceptional seasonal vegetarian menus crafted from local farm harvests.

**What's the average cost of eating out in Napier?**
Expect to pay **$18–$24 NZD** for a cafe brunch with specialty coffee, **$30–$45 NZD** for a vineyard lawn platter with wine, and **$65–$95 NZD per person** for a sit-down dinner with wine at a premier bistro (degustations at *Pacifica* run around $140–$160 NZD).
