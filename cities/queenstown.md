# Queenstown (Tāhuna)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `queenstown`
* **Island & Region**: South Island | Otago
* **Vibe**: The adventure capital of the world, dramatic Remarkables mountain range, glacial Lake Wakatipu, fine alpine dining.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Skyline Gondola & Luge (Bob's Peak)**: Steepest cable car in Southern Hemisphere with downhill gravity-fueled luge tracks and iconic town vista.
- **Lake Wakatipu & TSS Earnslaw**: 112-year-old vintage steamship cruising to Walter Peak High Country Farm.
- **Kawarau Bridge Bungy & Nevis Bungy**: The birthplace of commercial bungy jumping (AJ Hackett) and extreme 134m canyon swings.
- **Ski Fields (Winter/Spring)**: The Remarkables, Coronet Peak, Cardrona, Treble Cone.
- **Historic Arrowtown**: Charming 1860s gold-mining town with preserved Chinese settlement and autumnal golden foliage.
- **Milford Sound & Glenorchy**: Day-trip gateways through Fiordland National Park.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Fergburger & Fergbaker* (World-famous gourmet burgers & venison pies on Shotover St)
- *Vudu Cafe & Larder* (Waterfront espresso and creative brunch)
- *Flame Bar & Grill* (Lakefront steakhouse and South Island meats)
- *Amisfield Restaurant & Cellar Door* (3-Hat degustation and Central Otago Pinot Noir)
- *Patagonia Chocolates* (Artisan gelato and hot chocolate on the lakefront)

## Practical Notes:
- Peak summer (Dec–Feb) and peak ski season (July–Aug) bring crowds and higher accommodation prices. Book rental cars and activities well in advance.
- Winter roads require snow chains over Crown Range and mountain passes.
