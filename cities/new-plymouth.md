# New Plymouth (Ngāmotu)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `new-plymouth`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Taranaki
* **Tier**: `Tier 2: Regional Thematic Specialist`
* **Primary Archetype**: Coastal surf culture meets cutting-edge contemporary arts, dominated by the perfect volcanic cone of Mount Taranaki.

---

## 🎯 Topic Compatibility & Feasibility Matrix:
* **Best Topics & Strengths**: Volcanic tramping & tarn reflections (Mount Taranaki / Pouakai Crossing), coastal walks & architecture (13km Coastal Walkway, Te Rewa Rewa Bridge), kinetic art & contemporary galleries (Len Lye Centre / Govett-Brewster), botanical wonderlands (Pukekura Park, summer Festival of Lights), surf road trips (Surf Highway 45), artisan coffee (Ozone HQ).
* **Limitations & Banned Angles**: Do not write "bustling subway metropolis" or "desert landscape". Mountain weather is volatile and changes in minutes; thermal layers required for hikes.

---

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Mount Taranaki / Egmont National Park**: Pouakai Crossing, mirror tarns, Dawson Falls, goblin moss forests.
- **New Plymouth Coastal Walkway & Te Rewa Rewa Bridge**: 13.2km seaside promenade with wave-shaped bridge framing Mt Taranaki.
- **Govett-Brewster Art Gallery & Len Lye Centre**: Curved stainless-steel facade, motorized kinetic sculptures and experimental film.
- **Pukekura Park**: 52 hectares of botanical gardens, subterranean fernery, rowboats, and summer Festival of Lights.
- **Surf Highway 45**: 105km coastal route connecting premier surf breaks (Stent Road).
