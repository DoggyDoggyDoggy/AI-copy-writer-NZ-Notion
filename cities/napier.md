# Napier (Ahuriri)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `napier`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Hawke's Bay
* **Vibe**: Art Deco capital of the world, Mediterranean climate, sun-drenched vineyards, coastal cycle trails.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Art Deco Historic Quarter**: Downtown streets rebuilt in 1930s Art Deco, Spanish Mission, and Stripped Classical styles after the 1931 earthquake (Marine Parade, Emerson St, Tennyson St).
- **Marine Parade & Tom Parker Fountain**: Coastal promenade lined with Norfolk pines, National Aquarium of New Zealand, and viewing platform.
- **Te Mata Peak** (nearby Havelock North): 399m dramatic limestone ridge with 360-degree views over Heretaunga Plains.
- **Ahuriri Estuary & Historic Port**: Working fishing port turned dining, brewery, and boutique shopping quarter.
- **Hawke's Bay Trails**: Over 200km of flat, scenic off-road cycle paths linking city to wineries.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Mister D* (Famous for injecting custom custard/jelly into warm freshly fried brioche doughnuts)
- *Church Road Winery & Mission Estate* (NZ's oldest wineries with historic stone cellar doors and fine dining)
- *Brave Brewing Co.* (Top craft beer in Hawke's Bay)
- *FG Smith Eatery* (Ahuriri brunch favorite)

## Practical Notes:
- One of the sunniest spots in NZ. February Art Deco Festival transforms the whole city into the 1930s with vintage cars, costumes, and jazz.
