# Wellington (Te Whanganui-a-Tara)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `wellington`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Wellington Region
* **Tier**: `Tier 1: Metropolitan / Creative Capital`
* **Primary Archetype**: Compact creative capital, craft coffee & beer capital, indie cinema & visual effects, museum taonga, wild harbour winds.

---

## 🎯 Topic Compatibility & Feasibility Matrix:
* **Best Topics & Strengths**: Specialty coffee & micro-roasteries (Coffee Supreme, Flight Coffee), craft beer taprooms & experimental breweries (Garage Project), national museums & Māori taonga (Te Papa), film tourism & special effects (Wētā Workshop), urban wildlife ecosanctuaries (Zealandia), laneway dining & indie retail (Cuba St/Hannahs Laneway).
* **Limitations & Banned Angles**: Do not write "tropical beach resort" or "vast open highways". Wellington is hyper-compact, hilly, and notoriously windy. Umbrellas are useless.

---

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Museum of New Zealand Te Papa Tongarewa**: Free national museum with groundbreaking interactive exhibits (Gallipoli, Māori taonga).
- **Wellington Cable Car & Botanic Garden**: Historic red funicular rising from Lambton Quay to Kelburn lookout.
- **Cuba Street & Hannahs Laneway**: Bohemian pedestrian spine with Bucket Fountain, street buskers, indie boutiques, and artisan chocolate/craft beer.
- **Zealandia Te Māra a Tāne**: Fully-fenced 225-hectare urban ecosanctuary with wild kiwi, takahē, kākā, and tuatara.
- **Wētā Workshop**: Film visual effects, armor forging, and props tour in Miramar.

## 💡 Honest E-E-A-T Realities:
- High winds are frequent ("Windy Welly"); pack a windproof jacket. Highly walkable city centre—no car needed downtown.
