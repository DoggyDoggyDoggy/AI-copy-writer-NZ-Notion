# Invercargill (Waihōpai)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `invercargill`
* **Island & Region**: South Island | Southland
* **Vibe**: Southern hospitality, classic motoring heritage, wide streets, gateway to Stewart Island and The Catlins.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Bill Richardson Transport World & Classic Motorcycle Mecca**: World-class vintage truck and motorbike museums.
- **Queens Park**: 80-hectare park with aviary, animal enclosures, rose garden, and Japanese garden.
- **E. Hayes and Sons**: Hardware store housing Burt Munro's original "World's Fastest Indian" motorcycle.
- **Oreti Beach**: 26km long drivable beach where Burt Munro tested his speed machines.
- **Bluff & Stirling Point**: Iconic yellow signpost marking the southern end of State Highway 1, world-famous Bluff oysters (season: March–August).

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *The Batch Cafe* (Hearty Southland cheese rolls, artisan baking, top coffee)
- *Level One Restaurant & Bar* (Local Stewart Island salmon, Southland lamb)
- *The Cheeky Llama Cafe* (Located right inside Queens Park)
- *Oyster Cove (Bluff)* (Overlooking Foveaux Strait, fresh Bluff oysters)

## Practical Notes:
- Cool southern climate; pack thermal layers and wind-resistant outerwear year-round.
- Don't miss trying the legendary "Southland Cheese Roll" (affectionately called the *Southern Sushi*).
