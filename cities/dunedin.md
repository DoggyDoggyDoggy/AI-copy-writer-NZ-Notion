# Dunedin (Ōtepoti)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `dunedin`
* **Island & Region**: South Island | Otago
* **Vibe**: Scottish Victorian heritage, wildlife capital of NZ, energetic student culture (Scarfies), indie music & street art.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Otago Peninsula**: Royal Albatross Centre (only mainland breeding colony), Penguin Place (yellow-eyed penguins / hoiho), fur seals at Taiaroa Head.
- **Baldwin Street**: Officially recognized as the world's steepest residential street.
- **Larnach Castle**: New Zealand's only castle, surrounded by world-renowned gardens and harbour views.
- **Dunedin Railway Station**: Spectacular Flemish Renaissance architecture ("Gingerbread George").
- **Otago University & St Clair Beach**: Historic bluestone Gothic campus; surf break and heated saltwater outdoor pool at St Clair Esplanade.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Vanguard Specialty Coffee Co.* (Precision roastery, serious coffee focus)
- *The Perc Cafe* (Historic central spot, hearty savoury muffins & sweet treats)
- *Emerson's Brewery* (Dunedin's craft beer icon with taproom and brewery restaurant)
- *Plato* (Harbour-side converted seafood restaurant with daily catch)

## Practical Notes:
- Cool southern climate; weather can roll in from the Antarctic quickly. Layer up with merino wool.
- Peninsula roads are narrow and winding—drive slowly when spotting wildlife.
