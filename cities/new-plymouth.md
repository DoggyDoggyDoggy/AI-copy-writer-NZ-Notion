# New Plymouth (Ngāmotu)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `new-plymouth`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Taranaki
* **Vibe**: Coastal surf culture meets cutting-edge contemporary arts, framed by the perfect conical peak of Mount Taranaki.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Mount Taranaki / Egmont National Park**: Pouakai Crossing, famous reflective tarns, Dawson Falls, goblin forests.
- **New Plymouth Coastal Walkway**: 13km paved sea-edge promenade, passing the iconic *Te Rewa Rewa Bridge* (shaped like a crashing wave/whale skeleton framing Mt Taranaki).
- **Govett-Brewster Art Gallery & Len Lye Centre**: World-renowned kinetic art museum behind a shimmering stainless-steel curved facade.
- **Pukekura Park**: 52-hectare botanical wonderland with fernery, lakes, illuminated boats, and summer TSB Festival of Lights.
- **Surf Highway 45**: Iconic coastal highway dotted with world-class surf breaks (Stent Road, Fitzroy Beach).

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Ozone Coffee Roasters* (Original headquarters of the global coffee brand)
- *Federal Store* (Retro bakery & cafe, famous cinnamon rolls and hearty breakfasts)
- *Social Kitchen* (Shared woodfired feast in a converted paint factory)
- *Shining Peak Brewing* (Craft brewery with a pledge to give back to local charities)

## Practical Notes:
- Mountain weather changes in minutes. If tramping Pouakai, pack warm thermal layers regardless of city forecast.
