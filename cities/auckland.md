# Auckland (Tāmaki Makaurau)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `auckland`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Auckland Region
* **Vibe**: Poly-metropolis between two harbours, 53 volcanic cones, cosmopolitan dining, black-sand surf beaches, island escapes.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Mount Eden (Maungawhau) & One Tree Hill (Maungakiekie)**: Stunning volcanic cones with sacred Māori pā terraces and 360° city views.
- **Waiheke Island**: 40-min ferry ride to world-class vineyards (Cable Bay, Mudbrick, Man O' War) and golden sand beaches (Oneroa, Palm Beach).
- **Piha & Karekare Beaches (Waitākere Ranges)**: Wild west coast black sand beaches, Lion Rock, surfing, and rainforest waterfalls.
- **Viaduct Harbour & Wynyard Quarter**: Waterfront dining promenade, luxury yachts, seafood restaurants.
- **Ponsonby Road & Karangahape (K') Road**: Hip neighbourhoods with boutique shopping, trendy cafes, natural wine bars, and live music.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Daily Daily* or *Eighthirty Coffee* (Specialty coffee roasters on K' Rd and Ponsonby)
- *Depot Eatery by Al Brown* (SkyCity precinct: freshly shucked Mahurangi oysters, skirt steak, sliders)
- *Amano* (Britomart waterfront: handcrafted Italian pasta & artisan bakery)
- *Daily Bread* (Sourdough pastries, cardamom buns, and classic Kiwi meat pies)

## Practical Notes:
- Auckland traffic can be notorious on the motorway at peak hours (7:30–9:30 AM and 4:30–6:30 PM). Use the inner-city AT HOP card or ferry network where possible.
