# Where to Stay in Palmerston North: Best Central Areas & Suburbs

Situated on the fertile agricultural plains of the Manawatū and framed by the Tararua and Ruahine mountain ranges, Palmerston North (Te Papa-i-Oea) is a clean, green, and wonderfully unhurried university and provincial hub.

Because the city is built on completely flat terrain radiating outward from a 7-hectare central park (*The Square*), finding convenient, comfortable lodging is straightforward and budget-friendly. Whether you want a modern full-service hotel steps from cafes on Broadway, a quiet leafy motel near the rose gardens of Victoria Esplanade in Hokowhitu, or a practical highway motel along Fitzherbert Avenue, this guide breaks down Palmerston North’s best areas. ## 1. The Square & Central CBD: Best for First-Timers & Walkability

Centred around the sprawling green gardens, clock tower, and water features of *The Square*.

- **The Vibe**: Convenient and lively. Immediate access to George Street pedestrian dining, Regent on Broadway historic theatre, *Cafe Cuba*, and *Te Manawa* art and science museum.
- **Pros**: 100% walkable; you can stroll to all major shops, independent cafes, and craft brewpubs (*Brew Union*); very safe and flat.
- **Cons**: Can feel quiet on weeknight evenings after shops close.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Distinction Palmerston North Hotel & Conference Centre* (Premier 4-star full-service hotel right near The Square)
  - *Quest Palmerston North* (Modern self-contained serviced studio and one-bedroom apartments)
  - *Copthorne Hotel Palmerston North* (Reliable 4-star hotel on the fringe of the central park)

---

## 2. Fitzherbert Avenue & Hokowhitu: Best for Families, Nature & Quiet Stays

Running south from the CBD across the river toward Massey University.

- **The Vibe**: Leafy and prestigious. Tree-lined avenues leading to the 26-hectare Victoria Esplanade rose gardens, Peter Black Conservatory, and the He Ara Kotahi river pathways.
- **Pros**: Peaceful residential environment; dozens of high-quality motor lodges with spacious family kitchenettes; walking distance to the Manawatū River.
- **Cons**: A 10–15 minute walk to central evening restaurants on Broadway.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *BK's Premier Motel Palmerston North* (Upscale, modern 4.5-star motel units with spa baths)
  - *Fitzherbert Castle Motel* (Unique Tudor-style heritage motel with family suites)
  - *Aubrey Court Motor Lodge* (Quiet, clean motel units near Victoria Esplanade)

---

## 3. Terrace End: Best for Value & Highway Access

Located at the northeastern end of Main Street and Broadway.

- **The Vibe**: Practical, comfortable, and suburban.
- **Pros**: Seamless driving access to State Highway 3 heading toward Hawke's Bay and the Manawatū Gorge; exceptional lodging value; free on-site parking at all properties.
- **Cons**: Requires a short 3-minute drive or bus ride to reach central CBD dining.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Camelot Motor Lodge* (Comfortable, quiet units with outdoor swimming pool and spa)
  - *Carramar Motor Inn* (Spacious self-catering motel suites with on-site restaurant)

## Parking & Transit Logistics for Palmerston North

- **Free and Easy Parking**: Palmerston North is one of the easiest cities in New Zealand for motorists. Almost all accommodation options offer free off-street parking.
- **Bicycle Friendly**: The city is completely flat with dedicated cycle lanes. Exploring from the CBD to Massey University along the river pathway is best done on two wheels.

## Where to Stay in Palmerston North: Straight Answers

**How many days should I stay in Palmerston North?**
**1 to 2 days** is ideal. Dedicate one day to exploring Victoria Esplanade, Te Manawa museum, and the Manawatū Gorge Tawa Loop walk, and use Palmerston North as an economical transit base.

**Is it better to stay in the CBD or along Fitzherbert Avenue?**
Stay in the **CBD** if you want to walk to evening dining, craft beer pubs, and shopping. Choose **Fitzherbert Avenue / Hokowhitu** if you prefer a peaceful night’s sleep, are travelling with family, or want to be close to the river gardens.

**How far in advance should I book Palmerston North accommodation?**
Book **1 to 2 months in advance** if visiting during major university graduations (May and November) or national stockcar and agricultural events at Central Energy Trust Arena.
