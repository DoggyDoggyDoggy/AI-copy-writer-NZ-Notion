# Hamilton (Kirikiriroa)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `hamilton`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Waikato
* **Tier**: `Tier 2: Regional Hub & River City`
* **Primary Archetype**: Waikato River trails, internationally awarded themed gardens, fertile farmland bounty, gateway hub to Hobbiton, Waitomo Caves, and Raglan surf.

---

## 🎯 Topic Compatibility & Feasibility Matrix:
* **Best Topics & Strengths**: Award-winning botanical collections (Hamilton Gardens & traditional Māori Te Parapara), riverside paths & cycling (Waikato River Trails, Victoria on the River), specialty coffee & ice cream cult birthplace (Rocket Coffee, Duck Island), central road-trip hub to Hobbiton & Waitomo Caves.
* **Limitations & Banned Angles**: Do not write "ocean surf city" (Hamilton is inland; Raglan is 45 mins west) or "alpine ski base". Acknowledge its grounded, relaxed provincial character.

---

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Hamilton Gardens**: Internationally awarded themed garden collections (Italian Renaissance, Japanese Garden, Traditional Māori Te Parapara, Surrealist Garden).
- **Waikato River Trails & Victoria on the River**: Terraced CBD river park and paved walking/cycling paths along NZ's longest river.
- **Waikato Museum & ArtsPost**: Māori taonga, 200-year-old carved war canoe *Te Winika*, contemporary art.
- **Hamilton Lake Domain (Lake Rotoroa)**: 3.8km peaceful loop walk.

---

## ☕ Local Kai & Coffee Anchors:
- *Rocket Coffee Roasters* (Pioneering Waikato specialty roastery on Barton St)
- *Hayes Common* (River-view neighbourhood eatery and seasonal brunch on Jellicoe Dr)
- *Duck Island Ice Cream Flagship* (Birthplace of NZ's cult gourmet ice cream on Grey St)
- *Madam Woo* (Malaysian hawker dining on Victoria St)

## 💡 Honest E-E-A-T Realities:
- The Waikato River is considered an ancestral ancestor (*awa tupuna*) by Waikato Tainui iwi. The gardens are best visited on weekday mornings to avoid coach tour crowds.
