# Rotorua (Te Rotorua-nui-a-Kahumatamomoe)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `rotorua`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Bay of Plenty
* **Tier**: `Tier 2: Regional Thematic Specialist`
* **Primary Archetype**: Geothermal wonderland (Pōhutu geyser, mud pools, mineral steam), living Māori cultural heartland (Te Arawa iwi), world-class redwood mountain biking.

---

## 🎯 Topic Compatibility & Feasibility Matrix:
* **Best Topics & Strengths**: Geothermal phenomena & mineral hot spring spas (Polynesian Spa, Secret Spot), living Māori cultural villages & traditional hāngī cooking (Te Puia, Whakarewarewa), mountain biking & forest canopy walks (Whakarewarewa Redwoods), volcanic lakes & volcanic history (Waimangu, Tarawera).
* **Limitations & Banned Angles**: Do not write "high-fashion shopping metropolis" or "white-sand surf beach". Natural sulphur scent is present throughout the city; highlight as authentic geothermal character.

---

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Te Puia & Whakarewarewa Geothermal Valley**: Pōhutu Geyser (erupts up to 30m), NZ Māori Arts & Crafts Institute carving/weaving schools, kiwi nocturnal house.
- **Whakarewarewa Redwoods Forest (Titokorangi)**: 160+ km mountain bike trails under 70m redwoods, Redwoods Treewalk suspended bridges.
- **Polynesian Spa**: Natural acidic (Priest) and alkaline (Rachel) mineral hot spring pools overlooking Lake Rotorua.
- **Wai-O-Tapu & Waimangu Volcanic Valley**: Champagne Pool, Lady Knox Geyser, world's youngest geothermal valley.
- **Kuirau Park**: Free public park with bubbling mud pools and steaming foot baths.
