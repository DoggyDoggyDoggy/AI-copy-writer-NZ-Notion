# Taupō (Taupō-nui-a-Tia)

* **Notion Slug / City**: `taupo`
* **Island & Region**: North Island | Waikato / Central Plateau
* **Vibe**: Giant alpine crater lake, thundering rapids, geothermal thermal springs, gateway to Tongariro National Park.

## Key Landmarks & Anchors:
- **Lake Taupō**: Australasia's largest freshwater lake (surface area the size of Singapore), formed in a supervolcano caldera.
- **Huka Falls**: 220,000 litres of water rushing per second over an 11m drop into a turquoise pool.
- **Mine Bay Māori Rock Carvings**: 14m tall Ngātoroirangi rock carving accessible by kayak, boat cruise, or sailing yacht.
- **Otumuheke Stream / Spa Thermal Park**: Free natural geothermal hot stream merging with the cool Waikato River.
- **Aratiatia Rapids**: Dam spillway opening creating wild rapids 4 times daily.
- **Tongariro Alpine Crossing**: World-famous day hike just 1 hr south.

## Local Kai & Coffee:
- *Storehouse* (Industrial-chic cafe with incredible fried chicken and specialty coffee)
- *The Bistro* (European-style bistro with local lamb and Central Otago wines)
- *Replete Food Company* (Award-winning bakery and brunch)
- *Taupo DeBretts Hot Springs* (Soaking tubs and family waterpark)

## Practical Notes:
- Alpine winds off the lake can be brisk even in summer; bring a windproof layer.
