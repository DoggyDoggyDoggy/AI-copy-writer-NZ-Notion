# Where to Stay in Queenstown: Best Areas, Suburbs & Alpine Lodges

Surrounded by the soaring, razor-sharp peaks of the Remarkables range and cradled by the crystal-clear glacial waters of Lake Wakatipu, Queenstown (Tāhuna) is the Southern Hemisphere’s premier luxury and adventure resort.

However, Queenstown’s compact alpine topography, steep hillsides, and strict parking enforcement mean where you choose to stay will dramatically shape your holiday logistics. Do you want to step out your door into the buzzing nightlife and cult burger queues of the town centre, enjoy tranquil mountain views from a family apartment in Frankton, or retreat to a historic gold-rush cottage in Arrowtown? ## 1. Queenstown Town Centre: Best for First-Timers & Car-Free Travellers

The compact, bustling lakeside grid between Shotover Street, Steamer Wharf, and Queenstown Gardens.

- **The Vibe**: Electrifying and vibrant. Street performers along the lakefront promenade, queues for *Fergburger* and *Fergbaker*, lake cruises on the vintage *TSS Earnslaw*, and dozens of world-class bars and bistros.
- **Pros**: 100% walkable; you never need to wait for a bus or taxi after dinner; immediate access to tour departure points, bungy shuttles, and lakefront fire pits.
- **Cons**: Premium room pricing; can be noisy near Shotover and Camp Streets on weekend nights; hotel parking is scarce and expensive ($35–$60 NZD/night).
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Eichardt's Private Hotel* (Iconic 5-star ultra-luxury lakefront heritage hotel with private fireplaces)
  - *QT Queenstown* (Chic, design-forward luxury hotel with panoramic lake views and *Reds Bar*)
  - *The Rees Hotel, Luxury Apartments & Lakeside Residences* (5-Star terraced lakefront luxury on Frankton Arm)
  - *Absoloot Hostel QT* (Top-rated lakefront boutique flashpacker directly on Beach Street)

---

## 2. Frankton & Remarkables Park: Best for Families & Practical Roadtrippers

Located 10 minutes east of the town centre at the eastern tip of Lake Wakatipu.

- **The Vibe**: Sunny, spacious, and relaxed. Directly beneath the colossal western face of the Remarkables mountain range, adjacent to Queenstown Airport and major shopping precincts.
- **Pros**: Substantially cheaper rates than the town centre; ample free parking; flat terrain; close to Pak'nSave and Queenstown Central retail; connected to town by the $2 Orbus network and Queenstown Ferry.
- **Cons**: Lacks the romantic, historic village atmosphere of downtown Queenstown.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Hilton Queenstown Resort & Spa (Kawarau Village)* (5-Star resort with water taxi link to CBD)
  - *Wyndham Garden Queenstown* (Modern, affordable serviced apartment suites near Remarkables Park)
  - *Sudima Queenstown Five Mile* (Contemporary 4.5-star hotel with on-site restaurant and free parking)

## 3. Historic Arrowtown: Best for Romance, Heritage & Wine Lovers

Located 20 minutes northeast of Queenstown in the Arrow River valley.

- **The Vibe**: A beautifully preserved 1860s gold-mining settlement. Tree-lined avenue of historic miners' cottages along Buckingham Street, gold-panning in the river, charming boutique cinemas, and gourmet dining (*The Dishery*, *Amisfield* nearby).
- **Pros**: Complete peace and serenity away from Queenstown party crowds; world-class autumn foliage (April/May); immediate access to Gibbston Valley wineries.
- **Cons**: Requires a 20-minute drive or $2 public bus ride to reach central Queenstown adventure activities.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Millbrook Resort* (World-class 5-star golf and spa luxury resort set across 650 acres of rolling hills)
  - *Arrowtown House Boutique Hotel* (Charming, private luxury bed-and-breakfast in the heart of the village)
  - *Arrowtown Holiday Park* (Family-friendly cabins and camping within walking distance of shops)

## Parking & Transit Logistics for Queenstown

- **The $2 Orbus Transit Hack**: The local bus network (*Orbus*) connects the Airport, Frankton, Queenstown CBD, and Arrowtown for **just $2.00 per trip** with a reusable **Bee Card** ($5 NZD).
- **Parking Warning**: Parking in downtown Queenstown is heavily enforced with steep fines for overstaying meters. If staying outside the centre with a car, use the park-and-ride bus facilities or confirm hotel parking in advance.

## Where to Stay in Queenstown: Straight Answers

**How many days should I stay in Queenstown?**
**3 to 5 days** is the recommended minimum. This gives you time for lake activities, day-tripping to Glenorchy and Arrowtown, touring the Gibbston Valley wineries, and taking a full-day excursion to Milford Sound.

**Is it better to stay in Queenstown town centre or Frankton?**
Stay in the **Town Centre** if you are car-free, want lakefront dining at your doorstep, and love vibrant nightlife. Choose **Frankton** if you are travelling with family, have a rental car, want easy supermarket access, and prefer lower accommodation rates.

**How far in advance should I book Queenstown accommodation?**
For the **Winter Ski Season** (July to September) and **Summer Peak** (December to March), book **3 to 6 months in advance**. Queenstown is New Zealand’s most in-demand resort and room availability becomes severely constrained during peak seasons.
