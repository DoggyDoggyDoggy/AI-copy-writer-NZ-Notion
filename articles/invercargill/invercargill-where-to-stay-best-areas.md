# Where to Stay in Invercargill: Best Central Areas, Motels & Southern Stays

Guarding the southernmost edge of the South Island, Invercargill (Waihōpai) is a peaceful, nostalgic sanctuary of wide streets, grand Victorian and Edwardian brick facades, and legendary southern warmth (*manaakitanga*). 

Because Invercargill serves as the gateway to Stewart Island / Rakiura, the wild waterfalls and fossilized forests of The Catlins, and the historic oyster port of Bluff, it is one of the most practical and economical road trip bases in New Zealand. Whether you want a comfortable hotel in the newly revitalized city centre near Queens Park, a quiet family motel with free parking, or an oceanfront lodge in Bluff close to the Stewart Island ferry terminal, this guide breaks down the best places to stay. ## 1. Invercargill Central (CBD): Best for First-Timers & Walkability

Centred around Dee, Tay, and Kelvin Streets and the newly developed *Invercargill Central* dining and shopping precinct.

- **The Vibe**: Clean, wide, and welcoming. Walk to the famous Burt Munro motorcycle exhibit at *E. Hayes & Sons*, artisan cafes (*The Batch Cafe*), and regional dining (*Level One*).
- **Pros**: 100% walkable; immediate access to supermarkets, bakeries, and city attractions; safe and uncrowded.
- **Cons**: Quiet on weekday evenings.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *The Langlands Hotel* (Premier 4.5-star contemporary luxury hotel in the heart of the new CBD precinct with rooftop bar)
  - *Ascot Park Hotel* (Expansive 4-star resort hotel with indoor heated pool, sauna, and lush parkland grounds)
  - *Kelvin Hotel* (Reliable 4-star central hotel with on-site restaurant and parking)

---

## 2. Queens Park Fringe & North Invercargill: Best for Families & Motels

Located directly adjacent to the magnificent 80-hectare Queens Park.

- **The Vibe**: Peaceful and green. Step out of your motel straight into formal rose gardens, rhododendron dells, the public bird aviary, and *The Cheeky Llama Cafe*.
- **Pros**: Exceptional value; spacious multi-room family motel units with kitchenettes; complimentary off-street car parking.
- **Cons**: A 10–15 minute walk to central evening restaurants.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Balmoral Lodge Motel* (High-rated 4-star modern motel units near Queens Park)
  - *Asure Townsman Motor Lodge* (Comfortable, quiet suites with spa baths and kitchenettes)
  - *Invercargill Holiday Park & Motels* (Family-friendly cabins and self-contained units with park amenities)

---

## 3. Historic Bluff: Best for Stewart Island Ferry Passengers

Located 25 minutes south of Invercargill at the terminus of State Highway 1.

- **The Vibe**: Rugged, authentic maritime port. Foveaux Strait crashing surf, wild oyster fleets, and the famous yellow signpost at Stirling Point.
- **Pros**: Immediate access to the Stewart Island / Rakiura passenger ferry terminal; oceanfront walking trails (Foveaux Walkway); fresh seafood dining (*Oyster Cove*).
- **Cons**: Limited nightlife and dining options; windy coastal maritime climate.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Land's End Boutique Hotel (Stirling Point)* (Boutique oceanfront hotel positioned directly at the end of Highway 1)
  - *Bluff Seaside Holiday Baches & Cabins* (Self-catering heritage stays overlooking the harbour)

## Parking & Transit Logistics for Southland

- **Car is Mandatory**: A rental car is essential to explore Oreti Beach (where you can drive onto the sand), Bluff Hill, Curio Bay fossil forest, and the Catlins waterfalls.
- **Free Parking Everywhere**: Parking is 100% free and easy across all of Invercargill and Bluff.

## Where to Stay in Invercargill: Straight Answers

**How many days should I stay in Invercargill?**
**2 days** is ideal. Spend one day exploring Invercargill's motor museums, Queens Park, and Oreti Beach, and dedicate the second day to day-tripping down to Bluff or taking the ferry across to Stewart Island.

**Is it better to stay in Invercargill or Bluff?**
Stay in **Invercargill** for better hotels, diverse dining, supermarkets, and family amenities. Stay in **Bluff** if you have an early morning 7:30 AM ferry departure across to Stewart Island.

**How far in advance should I book Invercargill accommodation?**
Book **2 to 3 months in advance** if visiting during the **Burt Munro Challenge** (February) or during peak **Bluff Oyster Season** (May/June), when thousands of motorsport and food enthusiasts visit Southland.
