# Where to Stay in Gisborne: Best Beachfront Stays, Suburbs & Wine Lodges

Located on the remote, sun-drenched East Coast of the North Island, Gisborne (Tūranganui-a-Kiwa) is the first city in the world to greet each new sunrise. Famous for world-class surf breaks, barrel-fermented Chardonnay vineyards, and a relaxed, welcoming coastal lifestyle, Gisborne is a destination where you come to slow down and unwind.

Because Gisborne is compact, choosing where to stay comes down to your personal travel rhythm: waking up directly on the dunes at Wainui Beach for sunrise surfs, staying in the central inner harbour near micro-breweries and restaurants, or retreating to a tranquil boutique lodge among rural vineyards. ## 1. Wainui Beach & Okitu: Best for Surfers, Sunrises & Couples

Located just 8 minutes east of the city centre across the Wainui hill.

- **The Vibe**: A classic, unpretentious Kiwi surf haven. A continuous 4-kilometre curve of golden sand facing the open Pacific Ocean, world-renowned surf breaks (The Pines, Schools), and the famous *Raglan Roast* garage coffee bar.
- **Pros**: You can wake up, pull back your curtains, and watch the very first rays of sunlight on planet Earth over the Pacific horizon; pristine, uncrowded beach walks.
- **Cons**: Open-ocean surf can have strong currents (swim between flags); requires an 8-minute drive to central Gisborne supermarkets and restaurants.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Wainui Beach Holiday Baches & Cottages* (Direct beachfront private rentals with sun decks)
  - *Tatapouri Bay Oceanfront Cabins (10 min north)* (Eco-friendly glamping tents and beachfront cabins where wild stingrays swim in the bay)
  - *Okitu Boutique Coastal Stays* (Modern designer apartments tucked behind the dunes)

---

## 2. Gisborne Inner Harbour & CBD: Best for First-Timers & Walkability

Centred around the junction of the Taruheru and Waimata Rivers.

- **The Vibe**: Relaxed provincial maritime. A sun-soaked harbour promenade connecting boutique hotels, the historic *USSCo Bar & Bistro*, and the bustling taproom of *Sunshine Brewery*.
- **Pros**: Walking distance to all city cafes, retail shops, and dining; scenic riverside walking paths; safe, sheltered environment.
- **Cons**: Urban harbour setting rather than open-ocean sand dunes outside your door.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Portside Hotel, Heritage Collection* (4.5-Star premier waterfront hotel on the inner harbour marina with swimming pool)
  - *Captain Cook Motor Lodge* (Clean, reliable, comfortable motel near the beach and town)
  - *Senator Motor Inn* (Waterfront motel units overlooking the inner harbour basin)

---

## 3. Ormond Valley & Rural Vineyards: Best for Wine Lovers & Luxury

Situated 15 minutes inland on the fertile river flats of the Waipaoa River basin.

- **The Vibe**: Tranquil, pastoral wine country. Surrounded by golden Chardonnay vines, citrus groves, and mature heritage trees.
- **Pros**: Total peace and seclusion; direct proximity to iconic cellar doors like *Matawhero Wines* and *Millton Vineyards*; exceptional private stargazing.
- **Cons**: A car is mandatory for all travel.
- **Recommended Stays**:
  - *Knapdale Eco Lodge* (Boutique luxury lodge nestled on a 32-hectare scenic rural estate with gourmet dining)
  - *Vineyard Cottages & Homestead B&Bs* (Private self-contained cottages among working grapevines)

## Parking & Road Trip Logistics for Gisborne

- **Car is Essential**: While the CBD itself is walkable, having your own vehicle is crucial for exploring Rere Rockslide, East Cape lighthouses, and remote coastal coves.
- **Abundant Free Parking**: Parking is free and stress-free throughout almost all of Gisborne, including beachfront car parks and hotels.

## Where to Stay in Gisborne: Straight Answers

**How many days should I stay in Gisborne?**
**2 full days** is ideal. Spend one day exploring the town, surfing at Wainui Beach, and sliding down the natural Rere Rockslide, and dedicate the second day to cycling or driving between Gisborne's historic Chardonnay cellar doors.

**Is it better to stay at Wainui Beach or in the CBD?**
Stay at **Wainui Beach** if your dream trip involves waking up on the sand, surfing, and watching sunrise with morning coffee. Choose the **CBD / Inner Harbour** if you prefer walking to dinner, craft breweries, and convenience.

**How far in advance should I book for Rhythm and Vines?**
If visiting over New Year's Eve for the world-famous **Rhythm and Vines (R&V)** music festival, book **6 to 9 months in advance**. The entire district sells out every hotel room, motel, bach, and holiday park for the festival week.
